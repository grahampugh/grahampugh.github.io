I was born in Ormskirk, Lancashire, United Kingdom, and I am currently living in Germany and working as a Senior Systems Engineer at Jamf.

Although I studied Geochemistry, and qualified with a [Ph.D. studying Glacial Meltwater Chemistry][1] at [Aberystwyth University][3] ([read it here!][2]), I didn't succeed in that career, but soon realised my true talents lay in IT support. I have been lucky to work supporting the computing needs of teachers and academics in scientific institutions pretty much ever since:

- IT Technician, Web Designer, Deputy Network Manager at [Kings of Wessex School, Cheddar, Somerset, UK][4] (2001-09)
- IT Support Technician, Subject Specialist, Mac Specialist at [Bristol University, UK][5] (2009-15)
- Systems Engineer at an Apple consultancy in Basel, Switzerland (2016-17)
- Senior Apple Systems Engineer at [ETH Zürich][6] in Switzerland (2017-23)
- Senior Systems Engineer at [Jamf][7] (2024-)

At other times, I like to run, hike, cycle, visit the bouldering hall, learn German, and travel the world.
